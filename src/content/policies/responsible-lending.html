Responsible Lending

New Money Lender Pty Ltd trading as The Squad Institute Finance ABN 19 653 707 138 Australian Credit Licence 536096 (“SQIF”) is committed to responsible product design and customer suitability when offering our Buy Now Pay Later (BNPL) Career Sponsorship product.

Our product is designed to support customers in funding participation in The Squad Institute Program and transitioning into higher income employment outcomes.

Suitability Assessment

At SQIF, we recognise that this product is different from a traditional loan. We assess whether the product is suitable for you based on your circumstances, needs and objectives.

We undertake reasonable enquiries to ensure:

  • The product aligns with your intention to undertake the Program;
  • You are likely to obtain Professional Work;
  • You are likely to earn above $50,000 within a reasonable timeframe;
  • You will be able to meet repayment obligations when they are triggered.

Buy Now Pay Later Career Sponsorship Product

SQIF offers a Buy Now Pay Later (BNPL) Career Sponsorship product with the following features:

  • Funding up to $20,000 for The Squad Institute Program;
  • 0% interest;
  • Repayments only required when income exceeds $50,000;
  • Repayments based on income above $50,000;
  • Weekly or fortnightly repayments aligned to your income cycle;
  • Payment deferral available under defined circumstances.

This product is not a traditional fixed-term loan. Repayment obligations are income-contingent and may vary over time.

Eligibility

To apply, you must:

  • Be at least 21 years of age;
  • Be enrolled in The Squad Institute Program;
  • Be an Australian resident, permanent resident, or hold a valid Australian visa;
  • Be willing to provide income updates and supporting documentation;
  • Intend to obtain Professional Work following completion of the Program.

We will not provide the product if:

  • You are unlikely to obtain employment or income above $50,000;
  • You are unwilling to provide income updates;
  • The product does not align with your needs or objectives;
  • The product is likely to result in financial hardship when repayments are triggered.

Information and Verification

As part of our assessment process, we may collect and verify information about your financial situation. This may include:

  • Bank statements;
  • Employment or income information;
  • Other documents relevant to your circumstances.

We may also request updated information during the life of your account to ensure repayments remain appropriate.

Flexible Repayments

This product is designed to reduce financial pressure:

  • Repayments only commence when income exceeds $50,000;
  • Repayment amounts adjust based on income;
  • Payment deferral may apply during periods of low income or hardship.

Transparent Service

SQIF complies with all relevant legislation and regulatory obligations. We are transparent about how the product works, including:

  • How repayments are calculated;
  • When repayments start;
  • Any applicable fees or charges.

You are under no obligation to proceed if approved.

ID Verification

We take fraud seriously. As part of the application process, we will verify your identity using the information you provide. This may include electronic verification checks.

Assessment Process

We may conduct checks to assess your suitability for the product. This may include a credit check where appropriate.

Our decision is based on:

  • Your current financial situation;
  • Your expected future income;
  • Your ability to meet obligations under the product.

Are you a responsible user?

While we are committed to responsible product design, it is also important that you use BNPL responsibly. You should consider whether this product is right for you and your circumstances.
