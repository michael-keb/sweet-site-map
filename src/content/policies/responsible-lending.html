Responsible Lending

New Money Lender Pty Ltd trading as The Squad Institute Finance ABN 19 653 707 138 Australian Credit Licence 536096 is committed to responsible product design and customer suitability when offering The Squad Institute Finance credit product (Product).

The Product is designed to support customers in funding participation in The Squad Institute Program (Program) provided by The Squad Institute Pty Ltd ABN 26 628 164 643.

Suitability Assessment

We assess whether the Product is suitable for you based on your circumstances, needs and objectives.

We undertake reasonable enquiries to ensure:

  • the Product aligns with your intention to undertake the Program;
  • you are likely to obtain professional work or earn above $50,000 gross annual income following the Program completion;
  • you will be able to meet repayment obligations when they are triggered.

Product Features

The Product has the following key features:

  • $20,000 flat funding solely for the Program;
  • 0% interest rate;
  • repayments only required when gross annual income exceeds $50,000;
  • repayments calculated based on gross annual income above $50,000;
  • weekly or fortnightly repayments aligned to your income cycle; and
  • payment deferral available under defined circumstances.

This Product is not a fixed-term loan. Repayment obligations are income-contingent and may vary over time.

Eligibility

To apply, you must:

  • be at least 21 years of age;
  • be enrolled in the Program;
  • be an Australian resident, permanent resident, or hold a valid Australian visa;
  • be willing to provide income updates and supporting documentation; and
  • intend to obtain professional work following completion of the Program.

We will not provide the Product if:

  • you are unlikely to obtain employment or gross annual income above $50,000;
  • you are unwilling to provide income updates;
  • the Product does not align with your financial situation, needs or objectives; or
  • the Product is likely to result in financial hardship when repayments are triggered.

Information and Verification

As part of our assessment process, we may collect and verify information about your financial situation. This may include:

  • bank statements;
  • evidence of employment or income information; and
  • other documents relevant to your circumstances.

We may also request updated information during the life of your account, including income information and supporting documentation, to ensure repayments remain appropriate.

Flexible Repayments

This Product is designed to reduce financial pressure through the following repayment features:

  • repayments only commence when your gross annual income exceeds $50,000;
  • repayment amounts adjust based on your income;
  • repayments can be made weekly or fortnightly in line with your income cycle; and
  • repayments may be deferred during periods of low income or hardship.

Transparent Service

We comply with all relevant legislation and regulatory obligations. We are transparent about how the Product works, including:

  • how repayments are calculated;
  • when repayments start; and
  • any applicable fees or charges, including direct debit dishonour fees and any administration fee that may apply if the Product is not repaid within the initial 12 months.

You are under no obligation to proceed once approved, until you sign the credit documentation.

ID Verification

As part of the application process, we will verify your identity using the information you provide. This may include electronic verification checks.

Assessment Process

We may conduct checks to assess your suitability for the Product. This may include a credit check where appropriate.

Our decision is based on:

  • your current financial situation;
  • your expected future income; and
  • your ability to meet obligations under the Product.

Are you a responsible user?

While we are committed to responsible product design, it is also important that you use the Product responsibly. You should consider whether this Product is right for you and your circumstances.
