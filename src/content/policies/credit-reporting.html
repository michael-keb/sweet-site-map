Introduction

This is the credit reporting policy of New Money Lender Pty Ltd trading as The Squad Institute Finance ABN 19 653 707 138, Australian credit licence 536096 (“we”, “us” and “our”).

Words defined in the Privacy Act (Cth) 1988 (“Privacy Act”), the Australian Privacy Principles (“APPs”) and the Privacy (Credit Reporting) Code (“CR Code”) have the same meaning when used in this policy.

We also have a privacy policy, available on our website (see our Privacy Policy page).

Credit information that we collect

The kinds of credit information we may collect about an individual include information from a credit reporting body (“CRB”), such as:

  • verification of identification (name, aliases, date of birth, gender, current or last known address and two previous addresses, driver licence number);
  • consumer credit provided (credit provider name, licence status, type of credit, date entered into, repayment terms, maximum credit, date terminated or ceasing);
  • whether monthly payment obligations have been met, due dates, and payment dates;
  • a statement that an information request was made to a CRB by a credit provider;
  • type and amount of credit sought in an application where an information request was made;
  • payments at least 60 days overdue (over $150) where recovery is not statute-barred;
  • payment of previously overdue amounts;
  • financial hardship information;
  • arrangements with credit providers;
  • court judgments (non-criminal) relating to credit;
  • personal insolvency information;
  • publicly available credit-worthiness information (excluding certain court and NPII information); and
  • a credit provider’s opinion of a serious credit infringement.

Credit eligibility information

If we obtain credit reporting information from a CRB, or derive information that bears on creditworthiness and could be used to assess eligibility for consumer credit, we refer to that as credit eligibility information. It may include scores or assessments we generate.

How we collect and hold credit information

We collect credit information by telephone, in person, in application forms (including online), and when you make a request or enquiry. We may also collect it from public sources or third parties (e.g. referees, CRBs, other credit providers, insurers, or those selling or collecting debt).

We hold credit and credit eligibility information in secure electronic and physical files.

Why we collect, use, hold and disclose it

We may do so when permitted by law, including to:

  • assess an application for credit or a guarantor;
  • use a credit reporting body;
  • manage credit we provide;
  • help customers avoid default;
  • collect debts;
  • comply with laws;
  • deal with complaints;
  • assist other credit providers for similar purposes; and
  • other lawful purposes.

Disclosing credit information to a CRB

illion — Public Access Centre 1300 734 806, or email PAC.austral@illion.com.au. See also: illion credit reporting policy.

If we disclose to a CRB:

  • the CRB may include the information in reports to other credit providers; and
  • if you fail to meet payment obligations or commit a serious credit infringement, we may disclose that to the CRB.

Each CRB has its own policy on credit-related personal information — contact the CRB for a copy.

Your rights (Privacy Act)

Your rights include:

  • obtaining the CRB’s policy by contacting the CRB;
  • accessing credit-related information we hold, requesting correction, and complaining to us;
  • asking a CRB not to use your information for pre-screening for direct marketing;
  • asking a CRB not to use or disclose your information if you believe on reasonable grounds you are or may be a victim of fraud.

How to access credit eligibility information

On request we will provide details of credit eligibility information we hold, subject to exceptions (e.g. legal proceedings) under the Privacy Act. Unless unusual circumstances apply, we aim to respond within 30 days. We may charge a fee where permitted by law.

Requests must be in writing (letter or email), addressed to:

The Privacy Officer

Email: sqif@newml.com.au

We aim to respond within a reasonable timeframe.

Correction

We aim to keep credit information accurate, complete and up to date. You may request amendment in writing or verbally. Contact sqif@newml.com.au.

Complaints (credit reporting)

You may complain about conduct that may breach Part IIIA of the Privacy Act or the CR Code (a “Credit Privacy Complaint”). Specify the nature of the complaint. We will not charge you. Within 7 days we will acknowledge in writing and explain how we will handle it, then investigate. We may consult a CRB or another credit provider. Within 30 days (or a longer period agreed in writing), we will give you our decision in writing.

If you are not satisfied, you may use AFCA:
