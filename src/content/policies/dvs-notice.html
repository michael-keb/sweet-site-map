This notice explains how we collect, use, disclose and store your personal information to verify your identity document(s) using the Attorney-General’s Department’s Document Verification Service (DVS), only with your express consent.

We are bound by the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) (including the APPs), when applicable the Privacy (Credit Reporting) Code 2025 (CR Code), and the Identity Verification Services Act 2023 (Cth) (IVS Act). If you need this notice in another format or require assistance, please contact us.

Why is your personal information collected?

We are required by law, including the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act 2006 (Cth), to verify your identity before providing certain products and services. We collect your information to verify your identity in order to provide our products and services, including personal loans.

How will we handle your personal information?

We collect personal information through our website, online applications, email and phone. Your information is sent to the DVS Hub (Attorney-General’s Department) and matched against records held by the agency that issued your identity document. The Hub tells us whether the details match official records.

We retain copies of identity documents and verification results as required for legal and compliance purposes (often up to 7 years under record-keeping rules).

How will the Attorney-General’s Department handle your personal information?

The DVS Hub transfers information between us and the document issuer. The Hub does not retain personal information; the Department cannot view or edit information transmitted through the Hub for that purpose.

A managed service provider must meet APP and security requirements. Disclosure is limited to:

  • performing the contract with the Department; and
  • complying with requests under section 95C of the Privacy Act.

The Department is authorised to operate the Hub under the IVS Act.

Further information: Privacy Statement — Identity Verification Services.

How will the document issuer handle your personal information?

Your information is shared via the Hub with the agency that issued your document, to verify against their records. Those agencies already hold your information under their own policies and legal obligations.

What if you do not provide your personal information?

You do not have to use DVS. Alternatives may include providing a selfie with your ID, or video verification if we request it. If you do not provide what we need to verify identity, we may be unable to provide products or services.

Other disclosures

Where necessary we may disclose personal information to:

  • outsourced providers connecting us to the DVS Hub;
  • identity service providers;
  • credit reporting bodies;
  • customer service providers (e.g. call centres);
  • other credit providers and insurers;
  • government bodies as authorised or required by law;
  • debt collectors and assignees;
  • professional advisers (lawyers, auditors, accountants); and
  • law enforcement in certain circumstances.

We do not disclose your personal information to overseas service providers in the ordinary course; separate rules apply to verification assistance involving New Zealand (see below).

Verification assistance (Attorney-General’s Department)

If we need help verifying your identity, the Department may collect your information to verify documents through DVS and may disclose it to the relevant document issuer. This is authorised under APP 5.2(c) and section 27 of the IVS Act.

The Department handles information under the Privacy Act.

If your documents relate to other individuals (e.g. Medicare listing several people), we assume you have told them and obtained consent. Their information is used only for verifying your documents and is otherwise handled like your personal information.

There may be overseas disclosure to New Zealand where NZ agencies or organisations seek verification assistance for your documents.

If you do not provide information the Department requires, it cannot verify your documents through that process. More detail is in the Department’s Identity Verification Services privacy statement.
