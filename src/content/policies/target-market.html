Target market

The class of consumers in the target market are those who:

  • need $20,000 flat funding to pay for The Squad Institute program;
  • need credit for education and career development;
  • cannot or prefer not to pay program fees upfront;
  • want a no interest credit product; (Admin Fee ??)
  • want repayments flexible and based on income;
  • expect income to increase after completing the program;
  • may earn below $50,000 per annum or be unemployed;
  • want repayments aligned to their income payment cycle;
  • are willing to provide income updates and supporting documents;
  • are able to afford repayments once their income exceeds $50,000;(or minimum award??)
  • want clear, easy-to-understand terms;
  • want ready access to customer service.

Persons excluded from the target market

Consumers who do not meet our credit and eligibility criteria, including those who:

  • are under 18;
  • are not enrolled in The Squad Institute program;
  • need credit for personal or household purposes unrelated to the program;
  • prefer fixed repayments regardless of income;
  • are unwilling to provide income updates; or
  • are not resident in Australia.

Product key attributes

  • Loan amount of $20,000 flat;
  • 0% interest rate;
  • Income-contingent repayment structure;
  • Repayments based on income above the contract threshold (e.g. $50,000);
  • Weekly or fortnightly frequency aligned to income cycle;
  • Repayment commencement subject to employment/income conditions in the contract;
  • Direct debit;
  • Dishonour fee of $50;
  • Administration fee if not repaid within the initial 12-month period (as per contract);
  • No security or guarantor required;
  • Prepayment without penalty;
  • Payment deferral under defined conditions;
  • Clear, legible contract.

How attributes meet target market needs

Purpose

To fund participation in The Squad Institute Finance program and support career development outcomes.

Benefits

The product’s attributes benefit the target market because:

  • income-contingent repayments reduce pressure;
  • no interest reduces the cost of credit;
  • repayments commence when the income threshold in the contract is met;
  • flexible weekly or fortnightly frequency;
  • deferral can support hardship or low-income periods;
  • the product is designed for education and career outcomes;
  • application can be simple and online;
  • contract terms aim to be transparent.

Ongoing suitability

Will the product deliver what is promised? The structure is expected to remain consistent over its lifecycle and deliver outcomes aligned with its design.

Does the product need a redesign? As at the last review, no.

Does the target market need to be narrowed? As at the last review, no.

Distribution conditions

Responsible lending

The product is distributed in compliance with responsible lending obligations.

Voluntary conditions

  • only available to customers enrolled in The Squad Institute program;
  • no loans where credit and eligibility criteria are not met;
  • mandatory direct debit for repayments;
  • income verification at onboarding and during the loan lifecycle.

Channels

Distribution must not be misleading or deceptive, must not involve unsolicited credit offers, must contain required disclosures, must be reviewed where third parties are involved, and must follow written agreements where required.

Reviews

We intend to review this TMD on a quarterly basis. The responsible manager reports outcomes to the Board within two weeks.

We will also review if:

  • material product changes occur;
  • complaints, defaults, or hardship applications increase by 10% over 3 months; or
  • AFCA, ASIC or consumer groups indicate systemic issues.
