Target Market

The class of consumers that is the target market for our product are consumers who:

  • need funding up to $20,000 to pay for The Squad Institute program;

  • need credit specifically for education and career development purposes;

  • do not have the ability or preference to pay upfront program fees;

  • want a no interest credit product; (Admin Fee ??)

  • want repayments to be flexible and based on income;

  • expect their income to increase after completing the program;

  • may currently earn below $50,000 per annum or be unemployed;

  • want repayments to align with their income payment cycle;

  • are willing to provide income updates and supporting documentation;

  • are able to afford repayments once their income exceeds $50,000;(or minimum award??)

  • want product terms that are clear and easy to understand;

  • want ready access to customer services.

Persons excluded from the Target Market

Consumers who do not meet our credit and eligibility criteria, including:

  • are under 18 years of age;

  • are not enrolled in The Squad Institute program;

  • require credit for personal or household purposes unrelated to the program;

  • prefer fixed repayments regardless of income;

  • are unwilling to provide income updates;

  • are not a resident of Australia.

Product Key Attributes

The key attributes of the product are:

  • loan amounts up to $20,000;

  • 0% interest rate;

  • income-contingent repayment structure;

  • repayments calculated based on income above $50,000;

  • weekly or fortnightly repayment frequency aligned to income cycle;

  • repayment commencement triggered by employment/income conditions;

  • direct debit repayment method;

  • dishonour fee of $50;

  • administration fee applicable if not repaid within initial 12 months;

  • no security or guarantor required;

  • prepayment allowed without penalty;

  • availability of payment deferral under defined conditions;

  • clear, legible and easy to understand contract.

Distribution Conditions

Responsible Lending Obligations

The product will be distributed in compliance with all responsible lending obligations.

Voluntary Distribution Conditions

The product will be distributed in compliance with the following voluntary distribution conditions:

  • only available to customers enrolled in The Squad Institute program;

  • no loans to customers who do not meet credit and eligibility criteria;

  • mandatory direct debit for repayments;

  • income verification required at onboarding and during the loan lifecycle.

Distribution Channels

Distribution channels will present the product appropriately to consumers in that they:

  • do not contain misleading or deceptive conduct;

  • do not consist of unsolicited offers of credit;

  • contain all required legal disclosures;

  • are regularly reviewed where third parties are involved;

  • are subject to written agreements where required.

Scheduled Periodic Reviews

We will conduct periodic reviews of this TMD on a quarterly basis. The responsible manager will report outcomes to the Board within two weeks.

Other Review Triggers

We will review this TMD if the following occur:

  • material changes to the product;

  • increase in complaints by 10% over 3 months;

  • increase in defaults by 10% over 3 months;

  • increase in hardship applications by 10% over 3 months;

  • feedback from AFCA, ASIC or consumer organisations indicating systemic issues.
