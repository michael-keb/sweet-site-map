About This Document

This Target Market Determination (TMD) applies to The Squad Institute Finance credit product (Product) issued by New Money Lender Pty Ltd trading as The Squad Institute Finance ABN 19 653 707 138, Australian Credit Licence 536096 (we, our or us).

This document was last updated: 25 June 2026.

Target Market

The Product is for people who want to do The Squad Institute career transformation program (Program) provided by The Squad Institute Pty Ltd ABN 29 628 164 643 (Program Provider), and whose financial situation, objectives and needs are likely to match those set out below.

Likely financial situation

The likely financial situation of consumers in the target market is consumers that those consumers:

  • do not have the ability or preference to pay the Program fees upfront;
  • currently earn below $50,000 gross per annum or are unemployed;
  • expect to earn a higher income after completing the Program; and
  • will be able to afford repayments without hardship once their income exceeds $50,000 gross per annum.

Likely objectives and needs

The likely objectives and needs of consumers in the target market are that those consumers:

  • need credit of up to $20,000 to pay the Program fee solely for mentoring and career development purposes;
  • want a credit product with no interest charges;
  • want repayments to be flexible and contingent on income earned following completion of the Program;
  • want repayments to align with their income payment cycle;
  • want product terms that are clear and easy to understand; and
  • want ready access to customer services.

Persons excluded from the target market

The Product is not designed for people who:

  • require credit for personal or household purposes unrelated to the Program;
  • prefer fixed repayments regardless of their income;
  • are unwilling to provide income updates and supporting documentation; or
  • are not a resident of Australia.

Product Key Attributes

Key attributes

The key attributes of the Product are:

  • credit amount of up to $20,000, correlating with the Program fee;
  • 0% interest rate;
  • income-contingent repayment structure;
  • repayments calculated based on gross annual income above $50,000;
  • weekly or fortnightly repayment frequency aligned to income cycle;
  • repayment commencement triggered by employment/income conditions;
  • direct debit repayment method;
  • dishonour fee of $50;
  • administration fee applicable if not repaid within initial 12 months;
  • no security or guarantor required;
  • prepayment allowed without penalty;
  • availability of payment deferral under defined conditions; and
  • clear, legible and easy to understand contract.

Key attributes related to the needs, objectives and financial situation of the target market

The Product's key attributes are likely to be consistent with the likely objectives, financial situation and needs of consumers in the target market for the following reasons:

  • income-contingent repayments reduce financial pressure;
  • interest free reduces the overall cost of credit;
  • repayments only commence when income threshold is met;
  • flexible repayment frequency, weekly or fortnightly;
  • payment deferral supports hardship and low-income periods;
  • the Product is specifically designed for mentoring and career outcome purposes;
  • application process is simple and online; and
  • contract terms are transparent and easy to understand.

Eligibility criteria

To be eligible for this Product, a customer must be:

  • aged 21 or above;
  • a resident of Australia;
  • enrolled in the Program;
  • needing credit solely to pay for the Program fee; and
  • qualified under our credit assessment criteria.

Distribution Conditions

Distribution channel

The Product is distributed through:

  • our online website; and
  • the Program Provider.

Distribution conditions

The Product will be distributed in compliance with the following distribution conditions:

  • the Product can only be available to customers enrolled in the Program;
  • no credit can be provided to customers who do not meet credit and eligibility criteria;
  • the Product must be distributed in compliance with all responsible lending obligations;
  • the customer must agree to repayments being made only through direct debits;
  • income verification must be conducted at onboarding and during the Product lifecycle;
  • the customer may only apply for the Product through a process embedded within the Program website or platform operated by us, or through a click-through link directing the customer to our website. The customer must complete the Product application directly through the application flow or process provided by us;
  • any embedded application process or referral flow must be designed and operated in accordance with all applicable credit, privacy, disclosure and responsible lending obligations;
  • where the Product is distributed by the Program Provider:
    • information given to consumers about the Product must be correct, accurate and not misleading;
    • the Program Provider must not deal with, or provide any assistance or services to consumers in relation to, the Product, other than solely advising consumers that we provide the Product and including the click-through link to the Product on the Program Provider's website;
    • the click-through link to the Product on the Program Provider's website must direct consumers to a section of our website as advised by us; and
    • the Program Provider will not be able to assess, approve or recommend the Product; and
  • all credit assessment and approval decisions remain solely with us, with no involvement of the Program Provider or any third party.

Distribution determination

Distribution channel and conditions will present the Product appropriately to consumers in the target market in that they:

  • do not contain misleading or deceptive conduct;
  • do not consist of unsolicited offers of credit;
  • contain all required legal disclosures;
  • are regularly reviewed where third parties are involved; and
  • are subject to written agreements where required.

Scheduled Periodic Reviews

We will conduct periodic reviews of this TMD on an annual basis following the start date of 17 June 2026.

Review Triggers

We will review this TMD if any of the following events and circumstances occur which would reasonably suggest the TMD is no longer appropriate:

  • any significant dealings of the Product that are inconsistent with the TMD;
  • material changes to the Product;
  • increase in complaints by 10% over 3 months;
  • increase in defaults by 10% over 3 months;
  • increase in hardship applications by 10% over 3 months; or
  • feedback from AFCA, ASIC or consumer organisations indicating systemic issues.
