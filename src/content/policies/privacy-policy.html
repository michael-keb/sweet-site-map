Protecting your privacy

We seek to protect the privacy, accuracy and security of your personal information. We comply with the Australian Privacy Principles (“APPs”) in the Privacy Act (Cth) 1988 (“Privacy Act”) and, when applicable, the Credit Reporting Privacy Code (“CR Code”). Defined terms follow those instruments.

Personal information we collect and hold

We may collect personal information including:

  • name, current and previous address, landlord or mortgage details;
  • date of birth, gender, marital status;
  • government identifiers (e.g. driver licence, passport);
  • email and phone numbers;
  • financial information, credit history, bank or card details, references;
  • employment history;
  • sensitive information where relevant to insurance or financial hardship assistance.

We collect information about your accounts and transactions with us and when you use our websites (see Website below).

If another applicant is named in a credit application, you should only provide their information with their permission.

How we collect personal information

Mainly when you give it to us by phone, in person, or in documents such as application forms (including online), or when you make a request or enquiry. We may also collect from public sources or third parties (e.g. referees, credit reporting bodies or other credit providers).

Why we collect, hold, use and disclose personal information

We do so where necessary for our business functions or to comply with law. If you do not provide requested information, we may be unable to provide services at the same standard.

We may collect, hold, use and disclose personal information to:

  • provide requested information or services;
  • answer enquiries;
  • develop, administer and manage our services;
  • customise services;
  • assess applications and manage accounts;
  • compile profiles to serve you better;
  • engage CRBs for checks;
  • assess creditworthiness;
  • bill and collect debts;
  • statistics, research, marketing (including direct marketing), promotions and customer support;
  • notify credit providers of default or status of agreements;
  • handle complaints and enforce our rights; and
  • when authorised or required by law, including:
    • Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act 2006 (Cth);
    • National Consumer Credit Protection Act 2009 (Cth);
    • Real Property Act 1900 (NSW) and equivalents; and
    • Personal Property Securities Act 2009 (Cth) if we take security.

Disclosure to third parties

We may disclose personal information to:

  • related companies; distributors and introducers; CRBs; other credit providers and insurers;
  • service providers (mail, print, call centres, marketing, technology);
  • government bodies; guarantors and security providers;
  • debt collectors and assignees;
  • external dispute resolution schemes; and
  • professional advisers (lawyers, auditors, accountants).

We may disclose where required for the purpose it was collected, with consent, or as authorised or required by law.

Overseas recipients

We may disclose personal information to overseas recipients who provide services and lack an Australian link. If you consent, we may not be required to take reasonable steps to ensure the overseas recipient complies with the APPs. By using our services you may agree to disclosure to those providers — see the terms that apply to you.

Security

We handle and store personal information in line with the Privacy Act and take reasonable steps to keep it accurate, protect it from misuse, loss and unauthorised access, and destroy or de-identify it when no longer needed.

Website

We (or our providers) may log clickstream data such as:

  • IP address; top-level domain; pages accessed and downloads;
  • referring and exit sites;
  • browser and operating system; date and time of visit.

This helps us evaluate and improve site performance. The site may use session cookies for basic functionality. Cookies can often be deleted without affecting future sessions. Rejecting all cookies may still allow informational pages to be viewed.

Third-party sites we link to are not covered by this policy — check their privacy notices.
