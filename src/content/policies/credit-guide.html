Responsible lending

Under the National Consumer Credit Protection Act, we must not enter into a credit contract with you, or increase the credit limit of a contract, if the contract is unsuitable for you. A contract is unsuitable if, at the time it is entered into or the limit increased, it is likely that you will be unable to comply with your financial obligations, could only comply with substantial hardship, or the contract does not meet your requirements or objectives.

We must assess whether the contract will be unsuitable before entering into it or increasing an existing limit.

We must give you a copy of our assessment at no charge if you request it:

  • before entering the contract or before the credit limit is increased, if you ask before then;
  • within 7 business days, if your request is within 2 years of entering the contract or the limit increase; or
  • otherwise, within 21 business days.

We do not need to give you a copy if your request is made more than 7 years after entering the contract or the limit increase, or if the contract is not entered into or the limit is not increased.

Complaints

We have a dispute resolution procedure. Contact us first using the details above.
