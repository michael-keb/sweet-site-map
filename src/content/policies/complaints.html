New Money Lender Pty Ltd trading as The Squad Institute Finance is a customer-focused business. We aim to provide the highest standards of products and service. If you are dissatisfied with something to do with our products or service, we will do all we can to sort out the issue.

The Squad Institute Finance has a process for resolving complaints about our products and service. This process is provided free of charge. This document explains how we deal with complaints step by step.

Step 1 — How to make a complaint

If you have a complaint, please talk to us first. We are here to listen.

You can let us know in any of these ways:

  • through our website using Contact us;
  • by email to sqif@newml.com.au;
  • in writing to The Squad Institute Finance — Complaints, PO Box Q543, Queen Victoria Building, NSW 1230.

If you send us a complaint by email or in writing, we will confirm receipt within 1 business day of receiving it, or as soon as practicable.

If your complaint is about a credit default notice, hardship notice, or a request to postpone enforcement proceedings, we recommend you email sqif@newml.com.au to speak to a Customer Service Representative as soon as possible.

Need help?

If you need help making a complaint, email us at sqif@newml.com.au.

Step 2 — Quick resolution

We try to resolve complaints at the first point of contact. If we can resolve the issue to your satisfaction, we will do so immediately.

Step 3 — Investigation

Our aim is to resolve complaints by the end of the 5th business day after receiving the complaint.

If we cannot resolve the issue by then, the complaint will be referred to our Head of Customer Service, who will investigate.

The Head of Customer Service will write to you with the name of the staff member handling your complaint and any further information we need. You will be kept informed while the investigation is ongoing.

Step 4 — Informing you of the outcome

Within 30 days of receiving your complaint we will give you a written response that sets out:

  • the outcome of your complaint — either confirming the actions taken to resolve it, or that we have not accepted (or only partially accepted) the complaint;
  • if we have not fully accepted your complaint, our reasons; and
  • your rights to take the matter to the Australian Financial Complaints Authority (AFCA) and AFCA’s contact details.

If you are not satisfied with the outcome, we would appreciate discussing the matter with you.

Credit-related urgent complaints

Complaints involving default notices, hardship notices, or requests to postpone enforcement proceedings are urgent. We will provide a written response:

  • within 21 days of receiving the complaint; or
  • if we request further information, within 21 days of receiving that information; or
  • if requested information is not received, within 7 days from the date it was due.

Unauthorised transactions (PIN or access code)

For these complaints we will advise you of the outcome in writing within 21 days of receiving the complaint.

Complaints resolved within 5 working days

If your complaint is resolved within 5 working days, you will not receive a written outcome unless you ask for one, or the complaint is about hardship.

Step 5 — Delays

If we cannot prepare a written response within the 30- or 21-day period because the complaint is very complex or circumstances beyond our control cause delay, we will tell you before that period expires:

  • the reasons for the delay; and
  • your right to complain to AFCA and AFCA’s contact details.

Australian Financial Complaints Authority (AFCA)

If your complaint is not resolved to your satisfaction, you may lodge with AFCA: afca.org.au.

New Money Lender Pty Ltd is a member of AFCA. AFCA provides fair, free, and independent dispute resolution for financial complaints.

Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC)

If your complaint is about how we have handled your personal information, and we do not respond within 30 days or you are not happy with our response, you may complain to the OAIC. Complaints must be in writing and can be lodged online.
